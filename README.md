# ALBERT-Documentation

# CAD STATUS
Albert is a 14-servo animatronic consisting of 12 standard-size servos and 2 micro servos. While Albert is past its first prototype and is fully assembled, certain parts of the current CAD model require updates due to minor feature, sizing, and alignment issues. These tweaks will be addressed in time; meanwhile, any STL files with known issues are marked with "WIP" in the file name. 

Albert's most recent features include his monkey ears and tail. These do not have a standard method of attachment like the other parts of this assembly. Currently, I have Albert's ears glued to the front of the speaker holders on both sides of his head. The tail's "spine" consists of thick wire recycled from a clothes hanger and bent with a bench vise. The tail parts are currently strung onto the wire like beads and glued in place. I will be working on better integrating these parts in the future, along with making Albert more monkey-like. Right now, my focus has been on just creating a functional and clean proof of concept.
